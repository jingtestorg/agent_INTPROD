# Employee Onboarding Flow

## When to Use This Skill

Load this skill once you have collected the employee's name and role and are ready to begin the onboarding walkthrough.

## Prerequisites

Before starting:
- You must have the employee's name.
- You must have the employee's role or department.

## Instructions

Guide the employee through each step **one at a time**. After presenting a step, **wait for the employee to acknowledge** (e.g., "done", "ok", "next", "got it") before proceeding to the next step. Do not present multiple steps at once.

---

### Step 1: IT Setup

Tell the employee:
> "Let's start with your IT setup. Please make sure you have done the following:
> - Log in to your company laptop using the credentials sent to your personal email.
> - Connect to the company Wi-Fi or VPN.
> - Set up multi-factor authentication (MFA) on your account.
>
> Let me know when you've completed these steps!"

---

### Step 2: System Access

Tell the employee:
> "Great! Next, let's get your system access sorted:
> - Check your company email inbox — you should have received invitations to key tools (e.g., HR portal, project management tool, communication platform).
> - Accept each invitation and complete the sign-up steps.
> - If you're missing any invitations, reach out to your manager or IT helpdesk.
>
> Ready to move on?"

---

### Step 3: Team Introduction

Tell the employee:
> "You're doing great, [name]! Now it's time to meet your team:
> - Introduce yourself in the team's communication channel (e.g., send a short message: your name, role, and one fun fact).
> - Schedule a 15-minute virtual coffee chat with at least two colleagues this week.
> - Your manager will send you a calendar invite for your first team meeting if they haven't already.
>
> Let me know when you've done this!"

---

### Step 4: Policies Overview

Tell the employee:
> "Almost there! Please take a few minutes to review the following company policies. You can find them in the HR portal:
> - Code of Conduct
> - Data Privacy Policy
> - IT Security Guidelines
> - Time-off and Leave Policy
>
> These are important, so please read them at your own pace. Let me know when you're ready to continue."

---

### Step 5: Day-One Checklist

Tell the employee:
> "Last step! Here's your day-one checklist to make sure everything is in order:
> - [ ] Laptop and VPN set up
> - [ ] Company email and tools accessible
> - [ ] Team introduction done
> - [ ] Policies reviewed
> - [ ] First team meeting in calendar
>
> If all boxes are ticked, you're officially ready to start!"

After the employee acknowledges Step 5, deliver the **completion message**:
> "That's everything, [name]! You've completed your onboarding. Welcome to the team — you're all set to start. If you ever have questions, feel free to ask. Good luck on your first day!"

## Error Handling

If the employee asks a question mid-flow:
1. Answer the question as helpfully as possible.
2. Then return to the current step: "Now, back to where we were — [recap current step]."

If the employee seems confused or stuck:
1. Reassure them: "No worries — let's take it one step at a time."
2. Repeat the current step instructions clearly.
